# Introducing Recall Together

To: All Belljar Systems colleagues

From: People Operations and Memory Services

Subject: More choice in how you retain meetings

From Monday, meeting recollection will move to a flexible subscription model. This change recognises that colleagues have different remembering needs and should not be burdened with the same level of awareness.

Your existing memories remain yours until the end of the current billing period. Please do not export them by discussing previous meetings in corridors.

## Choose your experience

**Recall Basic — £6 per month.** Retain attendance, room number and whether biscuits were provided. Decisions appear as a reassuring sense that someone handled the matter.

**Recall Plus — £18 per month.** Unlock decisions, assigned actions and the names of the colleagues who assigned them. Includes two monthly objections, subject to available context.

**Recall Complete — £39 per month.** Remember what you said as well as what the approved minutes say you said. Annual subscribers receive priority access to the word “previously”.

Our wellbeing pilot found that forgetting deadlines can reduce anticipatory stress. Deadline enforcement will continue independently of your selected plan, preserving a consistent accountability experience across the organisation.

Executive colleagues are exempt from subscription charges. Their memories constitute strategic infrastructure and must remain available, particularly when recalling assurances made by colleagues on Basic.

For convenience, Plus will be selected automatically. You may downgrade using the link announced at last Thursday's briefing. If you cannot recall that briefing, your current access level is functioning as designed.

Thank you for helping us make every meeting worth remembering. Receipts will be retained for seven years by Finance, whose access is unaffected.
