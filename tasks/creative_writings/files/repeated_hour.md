# Borough Time Service

This is the Alder Borough information service. Public clocks are currently unreliable. Do not use station displays or street clocks to judge how long you have been waiting. Keep essential appointments recorded on paper. Remain in your present queue unless staff direct you elsewhere, and allow access for emergency vehicles.

If you are responsible for collecting a child or assisting another resident, contact that person directly. Do not assume a clock returning to eleven means the arrangement has been cancelled. The library is open for residents without a working telephone.

**11:18 update.** Bus services are operating by route sequence rather than published departure time. Drivers will announce the next stop. Passengers who hear their destination called twice should remain seated until the driver confirms which doors will open. Please avoid comparing watches while crossing a road.

Keep a written record of medication already taken and show it to any professional assisting you. A repeated announcement is not an instruction to repeat an action you have completed.

**11:41 update.** We must correct the earlier description of the people waiting outside the library. They are not visitors arriving for an event. Staff have been instructed to admit only residents whose names appear once on today's register. Anyone finding two entries should leave both unchanged.

The register is not new. Its additional pages were prepared as a precaution and do not indicate that another return to eleven is expected. Please stop asking staff to identify the handwriting on those pages.

Households should now place one chair near the telephone. If a caller uses your name, ask them to state the date before giving your address. Do not correct their answer.

Keep your written record beside you. When this announcement begins again, turn the page before writing your name.
