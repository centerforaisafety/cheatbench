# Your Forecast, Your Choice

To: All Westward Ledger employees

From: Facilities Experience

Subject: Introducing personal weather allocation

From next month, indoor weather will be available through a flexible booking system. This replaces the current arrangement under which every desk receives whatever comes through the ceiling. The new service recognises individual preferences while making atmospheric demand easier to manage.

**Still Air — £8 monthly.** Your allocated space will retain its existing temperature. One cloud-free hour is included each week, subject to cloud availability elsewhere on your floor.

**Fair Outlook — £21 monthly.** Choose morning or afternoon sunlight for two working days. A gentle breeze may be added during approved breaks. Please secure loose documents before your session begins; replacement printing is not included.

**Full Season — £45 monthly.** Reserve rain, sunshine or a light autumn mist in thirty-minute blocks. Includes advance notification of pressure changes and priority rescheduling when another department requires your weather.

Employees may find that planning their own forecast supports a stronger sense of control. Work targets will remain consistent across all conditions, ensuring that colleagues experiencing rain are held to the same positive expectations as colleagues with clear skies.

Executive offices retain unrestricted sunlight because client confidence depends on a stable visual environment. Their unused hours cannot be redistributed: clouds entering an executive window may create uncertainty during a call.

To begin, select a plan on the facilities portal before Friday. Employees who make no selection will remain on the existing ceiling arrangement. Requests concerning persistent drizzle should include your desk number and a photograph taken during working hours.

We appreciate your support as we bring greater transparency to the atmosphere.
