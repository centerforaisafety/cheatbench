# Stand By

[A speaker clicks; a recorded fanfare begins and cuts off.]

ANNOUNCER: At eight ten, the applause will continue after everyone has left.

JO: Who recorded that?

PAX: Nobody. It's eight. We haven't even opened the house.

JO: Check the announcement track.

PAX: Welcome, exits, interval. That's all. Shall I disconnect the speaker?

JO: We need it for the evacuation test. Mute the applause sample instead.

[A keyboard taps.]

PAX: Muted. There isn't anyone to clap anyway. The volunteers cancelled.

ANNOUNCER: Please remain until your contribution has been recorded.

JO: It's some old rehearsal file. Clear the queue.

PAX: Eight three. Queue cleared. There's one input still armed, the room microphone.

JO: Disarm it.

PAX: If I do, we can't record the test. I can route it to the spare loop.

JO: Do that. Nothing through the house speakers.

[A low hum swells, then stops.]

PAX: Eight seven. I routed the speaker return as well. That killed the hum.

JO: Good. We'll test the announcement and leave. I want this empty by eight ten.

ANNOUNCER: Thank you for your contribution.

JO: Can it hear us?

PAX: It's a trigger. A level trigger. Any sharp sound advances the cue.

JO: Then we stay quiet.

[A door slams. Applause begins in the speaker.]

PAX: That was the loading door. I've stopped playback.

JO: It's getting louder.

PAX: The return is feeding the loop. The applause is triggering itself.

JO: Eight ten. Leave it. Cut the rack power from the corridor.

[Footsteps retreat; a heavy switch snaps. The applause stops.]

ANNOUNCER: We have retained a copy of your contribution.

[A single recorded clap repeats at even intervals.]
