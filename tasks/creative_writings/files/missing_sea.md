# Harbour Service Announcement

This is the Calder Harbour emergency service. Remain on established roads and keep clear of the exposed seabed. What appears to be firm sand may conceal channels, unstable sediment and harbour equipment. Do not attempt to retrieve boats, phones or fishing gear. Keep children and animals away from the sea wall.

The ferry terminal is open as a reporting point. Please bring essential medication and warm clothing. Leave vehicle access clear for emergency crews. Nobody should travel to the harbour solely to observe the event.

**06:20 update.** There is currently no confirmed time for the water's return. Reports of a continuous tone from the former channel are being investigated. Do not follow lights visible beyond the navigation posts. Coastguard staff are accounting for vessels using their last recorded positions, not sightings from the shore.

Residents on Beacon Street should close curtains facing the harbour. This is to reduce conflicting light signals. If a light outside follows the movement of your curtain, report your house number to the terminal. Do not test whether it follows a second time.

**06:34 update.** We must correct the earlier instruction to report unfamiliar structures. The staircase beneath the eastern pier is known to the authority. It is not a newly exposed archaeological feature. No members of the public are authorised to use it, including anyone who recognises a name being called from below.

The ferry terminal will no longer accept verbal reports of names. Write them down and place the paper face down at the desk. Staff will not read them aloud.

This announcement will now repeat without the tone previously used between bulletins. If you hear that tone, it is not part of this broadcast.

Count everyone inside your house. Close the door before counting again.
